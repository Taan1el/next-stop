How to travel
W A S D
Walk. In the cab, W/S raises or lowers power.
E
Board nearby train. At the destination, deliver while stopped. Otherwise leave while stopped.
Space
Hold to brake. Begin braking before the platform.
C / R
Switch cab/chase view. Reverse direction while stopped.
Mouse / arrows
Click world to capture mouse. Drag to look if capture is unavailable. Arrow keys also look.
Esc / H
Pause / guide. Escape releases mouse.
F
Centre the train camera on the railway.
B / V
Add coal to the fire / sound the whistle. Ease power for bridges and crossings. Pressure supplies the engine’s pull.
J
Open the field journal. Discover places on foot along station platforms.
