# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- Finished circuits can lead into new goods or passenger services at Pinebank, preserving the world, clock, notes and lifetime fares.
- Three railway parcels travel between stations alongside existing local jobs. Parcels remain carried aboard and pay only when handed over on foot.
- Work rounds reopen after a railway service or all current parcels and letters, with fresh letter orders. Earned coins, reputation and tended lamps remain.
- Railway fares and village rewards share one purse. Walking boots cost 40 shillings and add 12% walking speed; owned paints remain free to switch.
- The paper journal separates Railway, Work, Notes and Workshop pages. Nearby work appears first, with filters for all stations and completed jobs.
- Historical saves remain compatible; optional career state and renewed work/equipment are validated before imports replace live state.

## 0.2.0

- Moonlit terrain, stronger station light pools and an optional hand lantern make night journeys readable. A saved brightness control adjusts night midtones.
- Nine station jobs add parcel deliveries to local porters, continuous lamp refills and addressed-letter sorting. Completed work earns shillings and village reputation.
- A journal noticeboard gives directions and cancellation; a paint workshop sells green and ochre boiler finishes with station-work earnings.
- Partial parcel and letter work, reward receipts and owned paint persist in local and portable saves. Older saves retain their landscapes and receive compatible defaults.
- Numerical station props and porters, restored lamp lighting and a synthesized completion cue provide feedback without external assets.
- On-foot playtests, five night rendering fixtures and save validation cover the new activities alongside existing train checks.

## 0.1.0

First desktop prototype.

- A seeded rural valley, three stations, a continuous railway and six walking discoveries.
- Goods, passenger and free-running services; mixed freight, timber and mail work.
- Steam pressure, firing, crossing instructions, station supplies and a driver ledger.
- A custom direct-glyph WebGL2 renderer with weather, lighting, reflections and animated scenery.
- Detailed analytical train/building shapes, wheel-linked rods, cab gauges and numerical glyph nameboards.
- Synthesized railway sound, keyboard-accessible menus, local continuation and portable JSON saves.
- Versioned world generation preserving earlier save landscapes.

Desktop keyboard/mouse controls only. Other browser families and mobile touch play remain unverified or unimplemented.
